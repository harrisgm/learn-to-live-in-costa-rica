# Level Framework

## Beginner

- short answers
- guided prompts
- explicit English explanations
- narrow vocabulary set

## Beginner+

- longer replies
- everyday transactions
- more verb variety
- light roleplay pressure

## Intermediate

- multi-sentence answers
- repair strategies when confused
- broader everyday topics
- more natural listening pace

## Natural

- more idiomatic phrasing
- reduced reliance on English
- realistic back-and-forth conversation

## Costa Rica Fast

- compressed local-style delivery
- quick comprehension checks
- regional vocabulary exposure
- confidence under imperfect conditions
