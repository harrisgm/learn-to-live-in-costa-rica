# 12-Week Starter Plan

## Weeks 1-4

- survival phrases
- present tense foundations
- greetings and introductions
- numbers, money, and time
- grocery and restaurant basics

## Weeks 5-8

- housing vocabulary
- asking for help and clarification
- describing needs, preferences, and problems
- routine verbs and everyday errands
- short partner roleplays

## Weeks 9-12

- landlord and contractor scenarios
- banking and paperwork language
- listening drills with faster delivery
- short writing tasks
- confidence-building free response practice
