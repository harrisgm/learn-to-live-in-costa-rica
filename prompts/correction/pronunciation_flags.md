# Pronunciation Flags

Review Spanish text and flag likely pronunciation trouble spots for English speakers.

## Focus Areas

- rolled `r` and tapped `r`
- `rr`
- `ll` and `y`
- `j`
- vowel clarity
- stress placement

## Output Format

- list the words that deserve attention
- explain why each word may be difficult
- suggest a short drill phrase

Keep it practical and concise. Do not pretend to hear audio if only text was provided.
